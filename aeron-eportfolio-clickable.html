About Me

Our family business is sewing. As far back as I can remember, our home has been filled with the sound of sewing machines, rolls of fabric, and customers coming in for gowns, jackets, and uniforms. Growing up in this trade taught me patience, precision, and the value of honest craftsmanship.

Today I help run Arjos Tailoring, where we customize laboratory gowns for medical students, produce alumni jackets and school uniforms under contract, and handle all kinds of clothing repair and alteration. Every piece that leaves our shop is made to fit and made to last.
